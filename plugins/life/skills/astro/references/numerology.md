# Indian Numerology — Chaldean System

Indian numerology uses the **Chaldean system** (not the Western Pythagorean system). Critical difference: in Chaldean, the number **9 is sacred** and is not assigned to any letter — but final totals can still equal 9.

## Chaldean Letter Values

| Value | Letters |
|---|---|
| 1 | A, I, J, Q, Y |
| 2 | B, K, R |
| 3 | C, G, L, S |
| 4 | D, M, T |
| 5 | E, H, N, X |
| 6 | U, V, W |
| 7 | O, Z |
| 8 | F, P |
| 9 | (none — sacred) |

## The Three Core Numbers

### Mulank (Moolank, Driver, Root Number)

The day of birth, reduced to a single digit.
- Born on the 1st, 10th, 19th, 28th → Mulank = 1
- Born on the 2nd, 11th, 20th, 29th → Mulank = 2
- Born on the 3rd, 12th, 21st, 30th → Mulank = 3
- Born on the 4th, 13th, 22nd, 31st → Mulank = 4
- ...and so on through 9.

The Mulank reflects the **innate personality and natural tendencies**.

### Bhagyank (Conductor, Destiny, Life-Path Number)

The full date of birth, all digits summed and reduced to a single digit.

**Example:** 27 / 04 / 1992 → 2+7+0+4+1+9+9+2 = 34 → 3+4 = 7. Bhagyank = 7.

**Master numbers (11, 22, 33)** — some traditions preserve these without reducing. In Indian numerology, most practitioners do reduce them, but it's worth mentioning to the user that 11, 22, 33 are spiritually significant if they appear before the final reduction.

The Bhagyank reflects the **life path and what one is meant to achieve**.

### Namank (Name Number)

Sum the Chaldean values of all letters in the user's full name (as they sign it / as on official documents — both can be calculated and compared). Reduce to a single digit.

**Example:** RAVI = 2+1+6+1 = 10 → 1+0 = 1. Namank = 1.

For a full reading, calculate Namank for: (a) full birth name, (b) commonly-used name, (c) spelling variations the user is considering. Sometimes a slight respelling can shift the namank into harmony with the Bhagyank.

## Meaning of Each Number (1–9)

### Number 1 — Sun (Surya)
**Traits:** Leadership, originality, independence, ambition, ego, willpower, pioneering.
**Career:** Authority roles, entrepreneurship, government, politics, management.
**Strengths:** Decisive, charismatic, self-motivated.
**Challenges:** Domineering, stubborn, lonely at the top.
**Lucky:** Sunday, Monday; gold, ruby; colors red, orange, yellow.
**Compatible numbers:** 1, 2, 3, 9.

### Number 2 — Moon (Chandra)
**Traits:** Emotional, intuitive, gentle, cooperative, diplomatic, dependent.
**Career:** Counseling, hospitality, nursing, teaching, water-related fields.
**Strengths:** Empathetic, peacemaker, imaginative.
**Challenges:** Indecisive, moody, over-sensitive.
**Lucky:** Monday, Friday; pearl, moonstone; colors white, cream, silver.
**Compatible:** 1, 2, 4, 7.

### Number 3 — Jupiter (Guru)
**Traits:** Wisdom, optimism, creativity, expansion, generosity, religious/philosophical.
**Career:** Teaching, law, advisory, religious, finance, writing.
**Strengths:** Knowledgeable, lucky, ethical.
**Challenges:** Over-confident, self-righteous, scattered.
**Lucky:** Thursday; yellow sapphire; colors yellow, gold.
**Compatible:** 1, 3, 5, 6, 9.

### Number 4 — Rahu (North Node)
**Traits:** Unconventional, rebellious, sudden changes, technology, innovation, restless.
**Career:** Tech, research, foreign affairs, unconventional careers, aviation.
**Strengths:** Original thinker, hardworking, independent.
**Challenges:** Erratic, controversial, lonely, prone to sudden reversals.
**Lucky:** Saturday, Sunday; hessonite (carefully); colors blue, gray.
**Compatible:** 1, 2, 5, 6, 8.
**Caution:** Combination of 4 and 8 (e.g., born on 4 with bhagyank 8) traditionally seen as challenging — needs extra spiritual work.

### Number 5 — Mercury (Budha)
**Traits:** Intellect, communication, adaptability, restlessness, business, charm.
**Career:** Business, sales, writing, accounting, media, brokering.
**Strengths:** Quick-witted, sociable, versatile.
**Challenges:** Restless, superficial, scattered.
**Lucky:** Wednesday; emerald; colors green, light blue.
**Compatible:** Almost everyone — 5 is the most flexible number.

### Number 6 — Venus (Shukra)
**Traits:** Love, beauty, harmony, luxury, artistic, family-oriented, sensual.
**Career:** Arts, fashion, beauty, hospitality, entertainment, cosmetics.
**Strengths:** Charming, refined, loving, peace-keeping.
**Challenges:** Indulgent, materialistic, possessive in love.
**Lucky:** Friday; diamond; colors white, pink, light blue.
**Compatible:** 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9.

### Number 7 — Ketu (South Node) — though some traditions assign 7 to Neptune/Moon
**Traits:** Spiritual, introspective, mystical, researcher, isolated, intuitive.
**Career:** Research, spirituality, occult, psychology, writing.
**Strengths:** Deep thinker, wise, unconventional.
**Challenges:** Withdrawn, melancholic, suspicious.
**Lucky:** Monday, Sunday; cat's eye (with care); colors blue-green, sea colors.
**Compatible:** 1, 2, 4, 7.

### Number 8 — Saturn (Shani)
**Traits:** Discipline, karma, hard work, delayed but enduring success, justice, suffering then mastery.
**Career:** Engineering, law, mining, real estate, politics, anything requiring perseverance.
**Strengths:** Disciplined, just, enduring.
**Challenges:** Cold, slow rewards, prone to obstacles, struggles in early life.
**Lucky:** Saturday; blue sapphire (only with proper consultation, can amplify negatives); colors black, dark blue, dark gray.
**Compatible:** 4, 6, 8 (with care).
**Caution:** 8 is the most karmically heavy number. Significant achievements come with significant tests. The Saturn weekday and discipline practices help.

### Number 9 — Mars (Mangal)
**Traits:** Courage, energy, fighting spirit, leadership, anger, determination.
**Career:** Military, surgery, sports, real estate, engineering, law enforcement.
**Strengths:** Courageous, determined, protective.
**Challenges:** Aggressive, impatient, accident-prone, conflict-attracting.
**Lucky:** Tuesday; red coral; colors red, crimson.
**Compatible:** 1, 3, 5, 6, 9.
**Note:** 9 is the perfect number — multiplied by anything reduces back to 9 (9×7=63, 6+3=9). Hence it's omitted from letter values.

## Compatibility — Reading Mulank-Bhagyank Together

A user with **Mulank 1 and Bhagyank 7** has Sun-Ketu energy: an outwardly leader who is inwardly a seeker. Mulank shows how they appear and act day-to-day; Bhagyank shows their deeper soul-purpose.

**Harmonious combinations** (similar or friendly planets):
- 1 + 3 (Sun + Jupiter): natural-born leaders with wisdom
- 2 + 7 (Moon + Ketu): emotionally intuitive, spiritual
- 3 + 6 (Jupiter + Venus): refined, fortunate
- 5 + 6 (Mercury + Venus): artistic communicators

**Challenging combinations** (enemy planets):
- 1 + 8 (Sun + Saturn): tension between ego and discipline
- 2 + 5 (Moon + Mercury): emotional vs. intellectual conflict
- 4 + 8 (Rahu + Saturn): the "self-destructive" pair, requires upayas
- 6 + 9 (Venus + Mars): love vs. anger

These are not deterministic — they describe the inner tension that the person works with throughout life.

## Practical Reading Workflow

1. Calculate Mulank from day of birth.
2. Calculate Bhagyank from full DOB.
3. (Optional) Calculate Namank from full name as user signs it.
4. Describe what each number means.
5. Describe the relationship between them — harmonious or tension-creating?
6. Suggest:
   - A favorable color, weekday, gemstone (with caveat to consult before wearing)
   - Lucky numbers for important decisions (matching Mulank/Bhagyank)
   - If a name change is being considered, what Namank would harmonize best with their Bhagyank
7. Always frame as "traditional indication," not "guaranteed truth."

## Lo Shu Grid (advanced — mention if user wants more)

A 3×3 grid (1–9) where digits from the user's full DOB are placed. Missing or repeated numbers indicate strengths and gaps. This is a deeper tool used in detailed numerology consultations and can be offered as a follow-up if the user is interested.
