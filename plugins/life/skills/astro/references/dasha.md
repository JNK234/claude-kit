# Vimshottari Dasha — Mechanics and Interpretation

## The Sequence (memorize this)

| Order | Planet | Years | Sanskrit |
|-------|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Ketu | 7 | Ketu |
| 2 | Venus | 20 | Shukra |
| 3 | Sun | 6 | Surya / Ravi |
| 4 | Moon | 10 | Chandra / Soma |
| 5 | Mars | 7 | Mangal / Kuja |
| 6 | Rahu | 18 | Rahu |
| 7 | Jupiter | 16 | Guru / Brihaspati |
| 8 | Saturn | 19 | Shani |
| 9 | Mercury | 17 | Budha |
| **Total** | | **120 years** | |

After Mercury, the cycle returns to Ketu.

## How the Starting Mahadasha is Determined

It is determined by the Moon's position at birth — specifically the Janma Nakshatra (the nakshatra the Moon was occupying). Each of the 27 nakshatras has a ruling planet:

| Nakshatras | Lord |
|---|---|
| Ashwini, Magha, Mula | Ketu |
| Bharani, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha | Venus |
| Krittika, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha | Sun |
| Rohini, Hasta, Shravana | Moon |
| Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishtha | Mars |
| Ardra, Swati, Shatabhisha | Rahu |
| Punarvasu, Vishakha, Purva Bhadrapada | Jupiter |
| Pushya, Anuradha, Uttara Bhadrapada | Saturn |
| Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati | Mercury |

The portion of the Mahadasha that has already elapsed at birth equals the portion of the nakshatra the Moon has already traversed (by degrees).

**Example:** Moon at 7° Mesha (Aries) is in Ashwini nakshatra (0°00'–13°20' of Mesha). 7° / 13°20' = ~52.5% traversed. So 52.5% of Ketu Mahadasha (52.5% × 7 years = ~3.67 years) has already passed at birth. The native enters the next Mahadasha (Venus, 20 years) at age 3.67.

## Antardasha (Sub-Periods)

Within each Mahadasha, all 9 planets get a sub-period (antardasha) in the same Vimshottari order, starting with the Mahadasha lord itself.

The duration of each antardasha is proportional:
> (Mahadasha years × Antardasha-planet years) ÷ 120

**Example: Mars Mahadasha (7 years) sub-periods:**
- Mars-Mars: (7 × 7) / 120 = 0.408 years ≈ 4 months 27 days
- Mars-Rahu: (7 × 18) / 120 = 1.05 years ≈ 12 months 18 days
- Mars-Jupiter: (7 × 16) / 120 = 0.933 years ≈ 11 months 6 days
- ...and so on through Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon.

Further subdivisions (Pratyantar Dasha, Sookshma Dasha, Prana Dasha) follow the same proportional logic and are used for fine-tuning event timing.

## Reading a Mahadasha — The Framework

When you have the user's current Mahadasha and Antardasha, walk through these questions:

### Step 1 — Strength of the Mahadasha lord

Look at where the planet sits in the natal chart:

**Strong placements (good period):**
- Own sign (e.g., Mars in Aries or Scorpio)
- Exalted sign (Sun in Aries 10°, Moon in Taurus 3°, Mars in Capricorn 28°, Mercury in Virgo 15°, Jupiter in Cancer 5°, Venus in Pisces 27°, Saturn in Libra 20°, Rahu in Taurus, Ketu in Scorpio)
- In a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) or trikona (1, 5, 9)
- In a friend's sign

**Weak placements (challenging period):**
- Debilitated (the opposite of exaltation: Sun in Libra 10°, Moon in Scorpio 3°, etc.)
- Combust (within 6° of Sun, except Saturn 15°, Moon 12°)
- In dusthana (6, 8, 12) — though 6 can give victory over enemies if lord is benefic
- In an enemy's sign
- Retrograde (vakri) — mixed; gives results unexpectedly

### Step 2 — What the planet rules in this chart

For each Lagna, planets become functional benefics or malefics:
- **Lagna lord** — always good when strong
- **Yogakaraka** — for some Lagnas, a planet rules both a kendra and a trikona, making it especially powerful (e.g., Saturn for Taurus and Libra Lagna; Mars for Cancer and Leo; Venus for Capricorn and Aquarius)
- **Maraka lords** — 2nd and 7th house lords give material results but can be challenging in their dasha
- **Badhaka lord** — for moveable signs Lagna = 11th lord; for fixed = 9th; for dual = 7th

### Step 3 — Antardasha lord interaction

The sub-period lord (Antardasha) modifies the Mahadasha:
- **Friend of Mahadasha lord** → period flows easily, similar themes
- **Enemy** → tension, conflict between the two themes
- **Neutral** → mild blend
- **Same as Mahadasha lord** (the first sub-period) → pure expression of that planet

### Step 4 — Dasha sandhi (joint period)

The last few months of one Mahadasha and the first few of the next can be turbulent. Major decisions are best avoided in the final 3 months of any Mahadasha or first 3 of a new one — let things settle.

## Planet-by-Planet Themes

**Ketu (7y)** — endings, detachment, spirituality, isolation, sudden events, research/occult interest. Material loss possible but spiritual gain. Health: cuts, viral infections, neurological.

**Venus (20y)** — relationships, marriage, luxuries, vehicles, arts, comforts, women. Best dasha for material refinement. Marriage often happens in Venus periods if Venus is well-placed.

**Sun (6y)** — authority, recognition, government, father, ego, vitality. Short but intense. Good for career promotion if Sun is dignified.

**Moon (10y)** — emotions, mother, public, fluctuations, travel, mind. Imagination and intuition strong. If Moon is afflicted, emotional volatility.

**Mars (7y)** — energy, courage, real estate, brothers, conflict, surgery. Action-oriented. Disputes possible; good for property purchase if Mars dignified.

**Rahu (18y)** — ambition, foreign things, technology, sudden rises, deception, obsession. Long and transformative. Career-defining for many. Ethical care needed.

**Jupiter (16y)** — wisdom, expansion, children, husband (in female chart), wealth, dharma, spirituality. The most generally benefic dasha. Education completion, marriage, children often in Guru dasha.

**Saturn (19y)** — discipline, hard work, longevity, delays, suffering then mastery, masses, servants. Longest dasha. Either makes or breaks. Karma-heavy.

**Mercury (17y)** — intellect, communication, business, education, friends, skin. Variable results depending on associates. Good for writers, traders, teachers.

## Other Dasha Systems (mention if user is curious)

Vimshottari is the default and most widely used. Others used in specific contexts:

- **Yogini Dasha** — 36-year cycle with 8 yoginis
- **Ashtottari Dasha** — 108-year cycle, used when Rahu is in a kendra/trikona from Lagna lord
- **Kalachakra Dasha** — based on Janma Nakshatra and pada, considered very predictive
- **Chara Dasha** (Jaimini) — sign-based dasha from Jaimini system
- **Shoola Dasha** — used for longevity assessment

## Practical Reading Workflow

1. State which Mahadasha and Antardasha the user is currently in (and approximate end dates).
2. Note where the Mahadasha lord sits in the chart — sign, house, dignity.
3. Note what houses the Mahadasha lord rules from the Lagna.
4. Combine these to give a theme: "This is a [career/relationship/spiritual/health] period because [reason]."
5. Note the Antardasha modifier.
6. Mention what's coming next (the next Antardasha or Mahadasha) so they can plan.
7. Suggest 1–2 supportive practices (mantra of the Mahadasha lord, a charity, a vrat).
