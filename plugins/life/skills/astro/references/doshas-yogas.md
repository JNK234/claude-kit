# Doshas, Yogas, and Standard Remedies

## Major Doshas (Afflictions)

### Mangal Dosha (Manglik)
**Definition:** Mars (Mangal) placed in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from the Lagna, the Moon, or Venus.

**Strength:**
- From Lagna alone — partial / mild
- From Lagna + Moon — strong
- From Lagna + Moon + Venus — severe

**Effects (traditional):**
- Marriage delays or conflict
- Aggression in partnership
- Possible widowhood for spouse if both partners are not Manglik

**Cancellation (Mangal Dosha Bhang):**
- Both partners are Manglik (cancels mutually)
- Mars in own sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn)
- Mars conjunct or aspected by Jupiter/Moon
- Native is born after age 28+ (effects diminish with age — traditional view)

**Remedies:**
- Hanuman Chalisa daily, especially Tuesday
- Red coral (with consultation)
- Mangala Gauri vrat for women
- Kumbh Vivah (symbolic marriage to a clay pot or peepal tree) before actual marriage — only suggested in severe cases and with family/priest involvement

### Kalasarpa Yoga (Kalasarpa Dosha)
**Definition:** All seven traditional planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) hemmed between Rahu and Ketu axis.

**Types (12, named by which house Rahu sits in):**
1. Anant 2. Kulik 3. Vasuki 4. Shankhpal 5. Padma 6. Mahapadma 7. Takshak 8. Karkotak 9. Shankhachud 10. Ghatak 11. Vishadhar 12. Sheshnag

**Effects (traditional):**
- Delays despite hard work
- Sudden ups and downs
- Mental restlessness
- Isolation, recurring obstacles

**Remedies:**
- Maha Mrityunjaya mantra (108 times daily)
- Rahu mantra: Om Bhram Bhreem Bhroum Sah Rahave Namah
- Visit Trimbakeshwar (Nashik) or Kalahasti (Andhra Pradesh) for Kalasarpa Shanti puja
- Donate sesame seeds, blankets, mustard oil on Saturday

### Sade Sati
**Definition:** The 7.5-year transit of Saturn through three signs — the 12th, 1st, and 2nd from natal Moon. Each sign Saturn occupies for ~2.5 years.

**Three phases:**
- 1st phase (12th from Moon): financial loss, distance from family
- 2nd phase (over Moon = "peak"): mental pressure, health, relationships
- 3rd phase (2nd from Moon): family/finance/speech tensions, sometimes results

**Effects vary widely** depending on Saturn's natal placement, friendship with the Moon's lord, and whether benefics aspect the transit. Sade Sati is not always negative — for Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, Taurus Lagnas (where Saturn is dignified), it can give significant achievements.

**Remedies:**
- Hanuman Chalisa daily
- Shani Chalisa, Shani mantra (Om Sham Shanaye Namah) Saturday evenings
- Donate mustard oil, black sesame, iron, blankets on Saturday
- Service to elderly, working-class, disabled
- Avoid arrogance and shortcuts; embrace discipline

### Shani Dhaiya (Ashtam Shani)
**Definition:** Saturn transiting the 4th or 8th house from natal Moon — each lasting ~2.5 years. Less severe than Sade Sati.

### Pitru Dosha (Ancestral Affliction)
**Indications:** Sun-Rahu conjunction, Sun in 9th afflicted, weak/afflicted 9th house, repeated family/lineage struggles, no children, or disturbed health passing through generations.

**Remedies:**
- Tarpan / Shraddha during Pitru Paksha (16 days in Ashwin month before Mahalaya Amavasya)
- Pind daan at Gaya
- Feed Brahmins, cows, crows on Amavasya
- Donate to elderly and orphans
- Tree planting (peepal especially)

### Guru Chandal Yoga
**Definition:** Jupiter conjunct Rahu or Ketu.
**Effects:** Wisdom corrupted; ethical confusion; over-ambition. Can also produce unconventional brilliance.

### Gandanta
Birth in junction nakshatras (last 3°20' of water sign / first 3°20' of fire sign): Revati→Ashwini, Ashlesha→Magha, Jyeshtha→Mula. Traditional shanti puja recommended.

## Major Auspicious Yogas

### The Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas
Five "great personality" yogas formed when the five tara grahas (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) are in **own sign or exaltation** AND in a **kendra (1, 4, 7, 10)** from Lagna.

| Yoga | Planet | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ruchaka | Mars in own/exalted in kendra | Warrior personality, courage, leadership, fame |
| Bhadra | Mercury in own/exalted in kendra | Intellectual brilliance, eloquence, wealth through wisdom |
| Hamsa | Jupiter in own/exalted in kendra | Wisdom, righteousness, respected, spiritual |
| Malavya | Venus in own/exalted in kendra | Beauty, comforts, artistic gifts, charisma |
| Sasa (Shasha) | Saturn in own/exalted in kendra | Authority, leader of masses, persistence, longevity |

### Gajakesari Yoga
Jupiter and Moon in mutual kendras (1-1, 1-4, 1-7, 1-10 etc. from each other). Gives wisdom, fame, social standing, family happiness.

### Raj Yogas
Combination of a kendra lord and a trikona lord (e.g., 9th lord with 10th lord). Indicates power, status, success.

### Dhana Yogas (Wealth Yogas)
Combinations involving 2nd (wealth), 5th (gains from past karma), 9th (fortune), 11th (gains) — particularly:
- 2nd lord with 11th lord
- 5th lord with 9th lord
- 9th lord with 11th lord
- Jupiter associated with Lagna or 2nd or 11th

### Vipreet Raja Yoga
Lords of 6, 8, 12 (dusthana) connected with each other. Counter-intuitively powerful — produces sudden rise after great struggle.

### Neechabhanga Raja Yoga
Cancellation of a planet's debilitation — e.g., the planet that exalts in the debilitation sign is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon. Turns weakness into kingly status.

### Budha-Aditya Yoga
Sun and Mercury together. Gives intelligence, communication skills. Common because Mercury never strays far from Sun.

### Lakshmi Yoga
9th lord in own sign or exalted, in a kendra/trikona, with Venus strong. Wealth and devotion.

### Saraswati Yoga
Mercury, Jupiter, Venus together or in kendras with each in own/exaltation/friend's sign. Brilliance in arts, scriptures.

## Gemstone Caution — Critical Rules

**Never recommend a gemstone casually.** Always say:

1. The stone should be for a **functional benefic** for the user's Lagna — not a malefic. Wearing a stone for a malefic for that Lagna can amplify harm.
2. Even when the planet is benefic, only wear if it's also **weak or afflicted** in the chart. A strong well-placed planet doesn't need amplification.
3. **Blue Sapphire (Neelam) for Saturn** — most powerful and most dangerous. Test for 3 days before committing. If anything goes wrong (illness, accident, loss), remove immediately.
4. **Hessonite (Rahu) and Cat's Eye (Ketu)** — should generally not be worn unless prescribed by a senior astrologer for specific dasha periods.
5. The stone should be of **specific weight in carats** (typically Lagna-lord's number ratti = ~1.8 ratti per carat). Activated through **mantra and proper muhurat** (specific day, hora, nakshatra).
6. **Substitutes** (uparatna) are gentler and safer than primary stones.

Always frame as: "Traditionally, [stone] is recommended for [planet]. However, gemstones are powerful — please consult a qualified Jyotishi before wearing one. A safer entry point is the corresponding mantra, charity, and rudraksha."

## Hierarchy of Remedies (suggest in this order, gentlest first)

1. **Mantra and prayer** — always safe, cumulative effect
2. **Vrat (fasting)** on the planet's weekday
3. **Daan (charity)** of associated items, ideally to the deserving on the planet's weekday
4. **Service (seva)** — to the population the afflicting planet represents (e.g., Saturn → working class, elderly; Mars → soldiers, brothers)
5. **Yantra** — specific to deity/planet, kept and worshipped
6. **Rudraksha** — bead corresponding to planet
7. **Visiting specific temples / pilgrimage** — Trimbakeshwar for Rahu, Kalahasti for Rahu/Ketu, Tirupati for Saturn, etc.
8. **Gemstone (ratna)** — only after consultation, last in this hierarchy because most powerful and most prone to side-effects.
